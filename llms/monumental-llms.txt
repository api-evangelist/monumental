# Monumental

> Monumental builds autonomous construction robots and the software that runs them. Its robots lay bricks and blockwork on real building sites, coordinated by Monumental's software platform (Atrium) and fleet operations (Fleet). The company operates across the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, and the United States and is venture-backed. As of this profile Monumental publishes no public developer API, SDK, or API documentation.
